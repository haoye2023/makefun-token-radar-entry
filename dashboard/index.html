How the model works

Implied value is TVL × category multiple × momentum × maturity × chain breadth, computed for every protocol from the snapshot's own figures — no name is scored by hand.

  • Category multiple — the median market-cap/TVL ratio of protocols in the same category whose token already trades, measured on this snapshot. Categories with fewer than three priced tokens use the global median, .
  • Momentum — the 7-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+60% and damped hardest of the four factors: one week of flow is the noisiest figure in the snapshot.
  • Maturity — years tracked with no token recorded: the factor rises from 0.75 to 1.25 over four years. The longer a large protocol has run tokenless, the more its launch is worth when it comes.
  • Chain breadth — +3% per extra chain beyond the first, capped at +24%.

The board holds pre-token protocols only. Wrapped, staked, bridged and versioned products of protocols that already have a token are excluded, as are exchange house products — they are not launches. Every protocol on the snapshot's watchlist is on the board; the watchlist is the floor, not the ceiling, so the board also carries the smaller early protocols a radar exists to find.
